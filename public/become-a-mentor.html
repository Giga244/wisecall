How to become a mentor

  1. 1
    Create an account and apply

    Sign up, then tell us your field, your price and how we can check your experience.

  2. 2
    Get verified

    Confirm your ID and show proof of your experience. In regulated fields we also check your license. A person on our team reviews each step.

  3. 3
    Start answering

    Once you are approved, you appear in Browse experts. Answer from your dashboard, and withdraw your earnings by bank transfer once your balance reaches 100 ₾; you add your bank account then.

The application: 5 steps, about 11 minutes

  1. About you about 2 min
  2. Your story about 3 min
  3. Price about 1 min
  4. Proof about 2 min
  5. ID check about 3 min

Have these ready

  • Your university or work page link, or a photo of a diploma or certificate
  • Your ID card or passport, for a selfie check (a phone is easiest)

If you teach at a university, your university page link is enough.
