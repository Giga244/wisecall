Your live session

Tickets are sold: only the description, the points, the cover and the time can change. If you change the time, everybody who bought a ticket is told and may cancel for a full refund.

What people will learn (up to 5, optional)

Live Q&A: up to 30 people, only you speak, questions in writing. Workshop: the same, and you can share your screen. Small circle: 2 to 8 people, everyone can use camera and mic.

At least 24 hours ahead. People see it in their own time too.

If fewer tickets are sold 6 hours before the start, the session is cancelled and everybody is refunded.
