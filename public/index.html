A question

I'm a marketing manager who wants to move into product design. Should I start with a course, or build projects first?

Product designer

Build first. Pick one real problem, design a small fix for it, and write down your thinking. Two solid case studies beat any certificate. A course helps once you know which gaps you have.

A question

I want to do a master's in Germany but my IELTS is 6.0. Should I retake it or apply anyway?

Studied in Munich

Check each program's minimum first: many accept 6.0 or 6.5, and some technical programs take English B2 without IELTS. If your dream program needs 6.5, retake it; one focused month on writing usually adds half a band.

A question

I got my first offer as a junior developer in Tbilisi. Is 1,800 ₾ fair, and can I ask for more?

Tech lead, 9 years

For a junior role that is the lower-middle range. Ask politely for 10–15% more and give one reason, like a project you built. The worst answer is 'no', and offers are almost never withdrawn for asking once.

A question

I'm a 5th-year medical student. How do I choose between cardiology and family medicine?

Cardiologist

Spend one week in each before deciding: the daily rhythm matters more than the subject. Cardiology means long training and night shifts; family medicine means more continuity with patients and a calmer schedule.

A question

I want to open a small coffee stand. What should I test before spending my savings?

Café owner, Tbilisi

Count people walking past your spot at the hours you'd open, on three different days. Then sell coffee from a table for one weekend before you sign a lease. Real sales will tell you more than any plan.

A question

I'm a freelance designer. How do I price a logo for a small local business?

Brand designer

Price the result, not your hours: offer a basic package and a fuller one with a mini brand guide. Most small clients pick the middle option, so always offer three.
