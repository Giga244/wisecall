    What an answer looks like

    Three examples written by the Wisecall team to show the kind of question people ask and the kind of answer they get. They are not real customers or real mentors.

    TechExample

    I'm self-taught and want my first developer job. Should I learn Python or JavaScript first?

    Pick the one that matches the jobs you can actually apply to. Look at ten local job posts, count which language shows up most, and start there. Depth in one language beats a little of both.

    Software engineer

    BusinessExample

    I'm launching a small product and have no competitors to compare prices with. How do I price it?

    Start from the value, not the cost. Ask five likely customers what they pay today to solve the problem, and price at a fraction of that. You can raise a price later, but it is hard to lower one.

    Founder

    TeachingExample

    My class of 25 teenagers talks over me. How do I get their attention without shouting?

    Choose one signal and use it every time: a raised hand, a countdown, or silence at the front. Practise it on the first day, thank the first students who follow it, and wait. Consistency matters more than volume.

    How we verify mentors

    Nobody appears in Browse experts just by signing up. Every mentor goes through these checks first. Self-taught experts and business owners are welcome: what counts is what they have really done.

    A real person

    A government ID and a live selfie, checked by our identity partner. Mentors must be 18 or older, and the name on the ID has to match their profile.

    Real experience

    Their story, a sample answer, and at least one piece of evidence of their work: a portfolio, a website, a company, a diploma or publications. We open the links and check them.

    Licensed where it matters

    Doctors, lawyers, financial advisers and other regulated professions also give us their license, and a person checks it against the official register before approving them.

    A person decides

    An automatic review helps our team, but a person makes every rejection and every decision in regulated fields. We may also meet a mentor on a short video call.

    Watched after approval

    A new mentor's first answers are checked before you see them. Reports and low ratings are reviewed, and we can suspend a profile at once.
