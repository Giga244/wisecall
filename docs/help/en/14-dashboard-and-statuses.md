# Your dashboard: what each status means

**My questions**
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Awaiting payment | You haven't finished paying at the bank (or it's still being confirmed). Nothing has been charged yet |
| Paid — awaiting answer | The mentor has the question. A countdown shows the days left (7 in total) |
| Being checked | The mentor answered; it's one of their first 5 answers and our team reads it first (up to 24 h) |
| Answered | Read the answer, then rate the mentor |
| Refund in progress | We asked the bank to return your money; your bank may need a few working days |
| Refunded | The money is back on your card |

Cancelled or failed attempts ("Payment not completed") are hidden behind **Show unfinished payments** and deleted after 7 days. Nothing was charged for them.

**Questions for me** (mentors): new paid questions with their deadline, and a form to answer. **Earnings** (mentors): on hold, payable, paid, and your payout history.

Everything important is also emailed to you, in the language you chose.
