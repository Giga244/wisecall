# How payment works

1. **You see the price first.** Each mentor sets their own price per question, in lari (between 5 ₾ and 2,000 ₾), or answers for free. That's the whole price.
2. **You pay by card on the bank's secure page.** When you press the ask button, you go to our bank's payment page (Bank of Georgia or TBC Bank). Wisecall never sees or stores your card number.
3. **Your question is sent only after the bank confirms the payment.** Usually that takes a few seconds after you come back to Wisecall. If the page says it's still waiting, leave it open a moment. It checks with the bank by itself.
4. **You have 30 minutes to finish paying.** If you cancel, the payment fails or you don't finish, **nothing is charged** and the question isn't sent. You can try again straight away; your text is still in the box.

**Free questions.** Some mentors answer for free: their profile says **Free**. There is no payment step: your question is sent at once, and the mentor has the same 7 days to answer. To keep it fair for everyone, you need a confirmed email, you can have at most 2 free questions waiting with one mentor and 5 in all, and each mentor takes a set number of free questions a week (when they are taken, the profile says "Free questions are full this week"). If the mentor doesn't answer in time, the question closes and you're told; nothing was charged, so there is nothing to refund.

**Where do I see my question?** In your **Dashboard → My questions**. Unfinished payments are hidden behind "Show unfinished payments" and deleted after 7 days.

**Who gets the money?** Wisecall holds it until the mentor answers. The mentor gets their share (80%) later, after the answer (see "How mentors get paid"). If the mentor doesn't answer in time, you get it all back.

**Currency.** You pay in lari. If your card is in another currency, your own bank converts it, and that conversion is outside our control.

**Before launch:** while Wisecall is in its pre-launch phase, you can't pay yet. You can join the waiting list and we'll email you once.
