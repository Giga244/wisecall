# Refunds

**You get your money back automatically, without asking, when:**
- the mentor doesn't answer within **7 days** of your payment;
- the mentor **declines** your question (it's outside what they can answer well, or against our rules): you see the reason;
- one of a new mentor's first answers is rejected by our team during its check;
- the mentor is suspended while your question is still open.

That's always the **full price**, including Wisecall's fee.

**After you've received an answer**
An answer is a delivered service, so disliking or disagreeing with it isn't a reason for a refund: an honest review is the way to say that. But if the answer doesn't answer your question, is wrong or could cause harm, is about something else, or is rude, use **Report a problem with this answer** in your Dashboard **within 7 days** of being able to read it. A person on our team reads the question and the answer and decides: a **full refund**, a **partial refund**, or **none**, and tells you why. The mentor's payout for that question waits until then.

**How long does it take?** We ask the bank for the refund straight away. The money goes back to the card you paid with; your bank decides how soon it shows on your statement (often a few working days). You get an email and a refund receipt when it's done. Until then your dashboard shows "Refund in progress".

**Please contact us before disputing the payment with your bank.** It's usually faster, and we can fix the problem first.

**Didn't pay in the end?** If you cancelled on the bank's page or the payment failed, you weren't charged, so there's nothing to refund.
