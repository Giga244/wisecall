# Refunds

**You get your money back automatically, without asking, when:**
- the mentor doesn't answer within **7 days** of your payment;
- one of a new mentor's first answers is rejected by our team during its check;
- the mentor is suspended while your question is still open.

That's always the **full price**, including Wisecall's fee.

**After you've received an answer**
An answer is a delivered service, so disliking or disagreeing with it isn't a reason for a refund. You can leave an honest review instead. We *do* refund in full if the answer is clearly spam, empty, off-topic, or breaks our rules. To ask for that, write to us **within 7 days** of getting the answer: use **Report** under the answer or the **Contact** page.

**How long does it take?** We ask the bank for the refund straight away. The money goes back to the card you paid with, and your bank may need several working days to show it ([CHECK: typical number of days with our bank]). You get an email when it's done. Until then your dashboard shows "Refund in progress".

**Please contact us before disputing the payment with your bank.** It's usually faster, and we can fix the problem first.

**Didn't pay in the end?** If you cancelled on the bank's page or the payment failed, you weren't charged, so there's nothing to refund.
