# Verification steps, and why we ask

Buyers pay for real experience, so we check every mentor before they appear. Here is each step and why it exists.

| Step | What you do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Email** | Type the 6-digit code we emailed you | So we can reach you and nobody uses your address |
| **ID + selfie** | Scan your ID card or passport and take a live selfie with our identity provider (Didit) | To make sure you are a real person, and the person you say you are |
| **Age 18+** | Nothing: it's read from your ID | Mentors must be adults |
| **Name match** | Nothing, if your profile name matches your ID | Buyers see your real name. Georgian and Latin spellings (ნინო / Nino) are matched automatically |
| **Experience** | The form, your sample answer and at least one piece of evidence | To see that your experience is real and fits your field |
| **Licence** (medicine, law, finance) | Licence or registration number, sometimes a document | These fields are regulated by law |
| **Review** | Wait | An automatic check (including an AI summary for our team) and then a person decides |

**Good to know**
- **Wisecall never receives pictures of your ID or your face.** The identity provider does the check and tells us the result, your verified name, date of birth and country. From the date of birth we only use "18 or older: yes/no".
- The automatic check opens your evidence links to see whether they show your name. Links that clearly show your name or brand help most. Avoid link shorteners and brand-new websites.
- **Nobody is ever rejected by the computer.** A person makes every rejection and tells you why.
- Sometimes we may ask for a short **video interview** if something is unclear. You pick a time slot in Settings.
- Uploaded documents are encrypted, only our team can open them, and they're deleted 90 days after the decision.
- Before launch, the ID step may say "ID checks open soon": you can fill in the rest meanwhile.
