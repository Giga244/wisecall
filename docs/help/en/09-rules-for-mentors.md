# Rules for mentors

**Answering**
- Answer within **7 days** of payment. Otherwise the buyer is refunded automatically and you earn nothing for that question.
- You answer **once**. It must be at least **150 characters** of real writing, and you can't edit it after sending. Take your time and check it first.
- Your **first 5 answers** are read by our team before the buyer sees them (up to 24 hours).
- If the buyer sends their one free follow-up question, reply within **3 days**.
- What makes an answer useful, with an example: [How to write a great answer](/help/write-a-great-answer).

**Honesty**
- Everything in your application must be true and your own: title, experience, evidence.
- Stay within your real experience. If a question needs a doctor, a lawyer or a financial adviser for that specific case, say so in your answer.
- An answer is advice from experience, not a diagnosis, a legal opinion or a personal investment recommendation.

**Never**
- ask a buyer to pay you or contact you outside Wisecall;
- share a buyer's question publicly or pass on their personal details;
- send spam or advertise unrelated services;
- ask for, pay for or trade ratings, or pressure someone to change one.

**What happens if rules are broken.** Buyers can report a mentor. Our team reviews every report. We may take a mentor off the site, refund their open questions and hold their payouts, always with the reason. If your licence expires, you're taken off the site until a renewed one is approved.

**Taxes** on what you earn are your responsibility.
