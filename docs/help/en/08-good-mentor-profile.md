# How to write a good mentor profile

Buyers choose in a few seconds. Your profile should answer: *who are you, what can you help with, and why should I trust you?*

**Title** (up to 100 characters). Be specific: "Backend engineer, 12 years in fintech" beats "Developer".

**Headline and "About me"** (up to 500 characters). Say who you help and with what: "I help junior developers plan their careers, prepare for interviews and design systems that scale." Mention results, not adjectives.

**Photo.** A clear, friendly photo of your face. JPG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB; we crop it square and remove hidden data such as location.

**Price.** Between 5 ₾ and 2,000 ₾. Start where a first-time buyer would say yes; you can change it any time (questions already paid for keep their price).

**Languages and city.** Buyers look for someone who speaks their language.

**Links.** LinkedIn and a website (https only). They also help your application.

**Your username** is your profile address: `wisecallge.ge/u/your-name`. You can change it once every 30 days; the old address keeps working.

**Reviews** come from real buyers only. You can reply to each review once, publicly. Keep it calm and useful. Never ask for, pay for or trade reviews.

Once you're listed, your profile is always public (buyers must be able to find you). Your email, payout details and documents are never shown.
