# Privacy and your ID data

**What we never have**
- Your **card number**: you type it only on the bank's page.
- **Pictures of your ID or your face**: the ID check happens at our identity provider (Didit), not at Wisecall.

**What we keep, and how**
- Your password only as a secure one-way hash (nobody can read it, not even us).
- For mentors, from the ID check: the result, your verified name, date of birth and country (**encrypted**). From the date of birth we only use "18 or older".
- Your IBAN and any licence number: **encrypted**. Only you and our team can see them; lists show just the last 4 characters.
- Documents you upload (licence, diploma): encrypted, visible only to our team, every opening is logged, **deleted 90 days after the decision**.

**Who sees your profile.** You choose in **Settings → Profile**: everyone, or only people you've had a question with. A listed mentor's profile is always public. Your email is never shown on a profile.

**Who else helps us** (under contract): our bank (payments), the identity provider (ID checks), our email service, our hosting provider, and an AI service that helps our team read mentor applications. The AI **never** receives ID data, your email, phone, bank details or documents.

**Cookies.** Only what's needed: staying signed in, a security cookie against fake accounts, and a short one during Google sign-in. No advertising or tracking cookies.

**Your rights.** You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion. Write to us through the **Contact** page. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.
