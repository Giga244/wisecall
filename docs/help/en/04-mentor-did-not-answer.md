# What if the mentor doesn't answer?

The mentor has **7 days** from the moment your payment was confirmed. Your dashboard shows a countdown on the question.

- About **2 days before** the deadline, the mentor gets a reminder email.
- If there's still no answer when the 7 days are up, **you get a full refund automatically**. You don't need to do anything, and you get an email.
- A mentor can't answer after their question has been refunded, so you'll never pay twice or wait forever.

**The answer is "being checked"?** A new mentor's first 5 answers are read by our team before you see them. That can take up to **24 hours** after the mentor answered. You get an email as soon as you can read it. If our team rejects the answer, you get a full refund.

**Still waiting and worried?** Look at your dashboard first: the status says whether the question is paid, answered, being checked, or refunded. If something looks wrong, write to us through the **Contact** page and mention the mentor's name and the date you asked.
