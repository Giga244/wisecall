# How to become a mentor

Anyone 18 or older with real, practical experience can apply. You don't need an employer, a degree or a job title: self-taught people, freelancers and business owners are welcome.

**The steps**
1. **Create an account** and confirm your email with the 6-digit code.
2. **Your application** (the "Become a mentor" button, or [the application](/apply.html)), in five short steps: about you (a headline, your field, years of experience), your story (100–1,500 characters), your price (5–2,000 ₾ per question, or answer for free), proof of your work (at least one link: your work, a website, LinkedIn, a publication, a company, a diploma…) and a quick ID + selfie check with our identity provider. See "Verification steps".
3. **Send it.** An automatic check runs first, then a person on our team decides.
4. **You appear on Wisecall once you're approved.** You need no bank account for that. **Your bank account comes later:** add the account holder's name and your Georgian IBAN in **Settings → Payouts** whenever you like, or right when you first withdraw what you earned. A mentor who answers for free needs no bank account.

Applying takes about 10 minutes if you have your links ready.

**Medicine, law and finance** also need a valid licence or registration number, which our team checks in the official register.

**Rejected?** A person always makes that decision and tells you why. Fix what they mention and apply again. There's no limit. You get an email as soon as it is decided.
