# Reporting a problem

**A problem with an answer or a mentor**
Use **Report** under the answer (in your Dashboard) or **Report this mentor** on their profile. Choose a reason (misleading, fake credentials, inappropriate, spam, or other) and add details if you like (up to 1,000 characters). Only our team sees who reported. One open report per person per mentor.

If you want your money back for an answer that is spam, empty or off-topic, report it **within 7 days** of receiving it (see "Refunds").

**A problem with a review**
Anyone signed in can **report a review** (abusive, false, personal information, spam, other). We hide reviews that break the rules. We don't remove a review only because it's negative.

**Anything else** (a payment, your account, a bug): use the **Contact** page. It goes straight to our team, and replying to our email reaches you. Tell us your account email, the mentor's name and the date if it's about a question.

**What happens next.** A person on our team reads every report and decides, with a written reason. [CHECK: our target reply time, e.g. within 3 working days.]

**In danger?** Wisecall isn't an emergency service. Call **112**.
