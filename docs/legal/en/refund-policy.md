## 1. When you get your money back automatically

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| You cancel on the bank's page, the payment fails, or you do not finish paying within 30 minutes | You are **not charged**. The question is never sent. |
| The Mentor does not answer within **7 days** of your payment being confirmed | **Full refund, automatically.** You do not need to ask. |
| One of a new Mentor's first answers is rejected by our team during its check | **Full refund, automatically.** |
| The Mentor is suspended or removed while your question is open | **Full refund, automatically.** |

A full refund is the whole price you paid, including Wisecall's fee.

## 2. After you have received an answer

An answer is a service that has been delivered, so it is not refunded because you disagree with it or did not like it. We refund in full
if, within **7 days** of the answer becoming readable, you report (with "Report" on the answer, or by writing to [support email]) that
the answer:
- is empty, meaningless, copied, or clearly written without reading your question;
- is about a different topic from your question;
- is abusive, discriminatory or breaks our Terms;
- asks you to pay or contact the Mentor outside Wisecall.

Our team reviews every report and answers within [OPS: 3 working days]. [LAWYER: check this against the Law of Georgia on the
Protection of Consumer Rights — right of withdrawal for digital services, and remedies for services that do not match the contract.]

## 3. How and when the money comes back

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through our bank. We ask the bank on the same day we decide; the bank then takes
[BANK: usually 1–10 working days — confirm with BOG/TBC] to return it, depending on your card's bank. You get an email when the refund
is complete. If the bank rejects a refund, we try again automatically and our team follows it up.

## 4. Chargebacks

If you dispute a payment with your bank instead of asking us, we will provide the bank with the question, the answer and the payment
records. Please write to us first: it is usually faster.
