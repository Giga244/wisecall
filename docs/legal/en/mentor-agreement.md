## 2. Verification and listing

2.1. To be listed you must: confirm your email; pass the ID and selfie check with our identity provider; have a profile name that

## 3. Answering questions

3.1. You set your price per question: a whole number of lari from 5 ₾ to 2 000 ₾. You can change it at any time; a change does not
affect questions already paid for.

3.2. You receive a question only after the bank confirms that the Asker paid. You must answer within **`ANSWER_DEADLINE_DAYS`
(7) days** of that confirmation. You get a reminder about 48 hours before the deadline. If you do not answer in time, the Asker is
refunded in full automatically and you earn nothing for that question.

3.3. You answer **once**. The answer must be at least 150 characters, in your own words, relevant to the question, and cannot be
edited after sending.

3.4. **Probation.** Your first `probationAnswers` (5) answers are checked by our team before the Asker can read them (up to 24
hours). If our team rejects one of them, the Asker is refunded and you earn nothing for it.

3.5. **No professional advice.** Your answers are general information based on your experience. You must not present them as a
professional service (medical diagnosis or treatment, legal representation, individual investment advice, etc.), and you must tell the
Asker to see a licensed professional when the situation needs one. You must not give advice that the law allows only licence holders
to give unless you hold that licence and it was verified by Wisecall.

has checked it.
