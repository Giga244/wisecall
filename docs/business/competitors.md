## Wisecall in one line

(ID + liveness + face match, 18+, name match, human review, licence checks in regulated fields, first 5 answers checked), Georgian + English,

## Georgia (local alternatives)

No direct Georgian equivalent of a verified, paid Q&A marketplace was found in web searches. People use:

| Alternative | How it works | Strength | Weakness Wisecall addresses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Facebook groups** (studying abroad, IT jobs, doctors’ residency, emigration, small business) | Free questions to large groups | Free, fast, many people | Unverified answers, contradictory advice, privacy (your question is public), no accountability |
| **Study-abroad and emigration agencies** | Paid packages, often free first consultation | Handle paperwork | Paid by universities or by selling services → conflict of interest; expensive packages when you only need one answer |
| **Consultants, lawyers, accountants** (individual websites, word of mouth) | Hourly consultations, usually in person or by phone | Professional responsibility | Hard to compare prices; scheduling; overkill for one question |
| **Tutoring marketplaces** used in Tbilisi (Preply, italki, Apprentus, TeacherOn, local tutor sites; e.g. Georgian tutors listed from about **50 ₾/hour** on tutoroo.co) | Book lessons by the hour | Established per-hour price anchors | Lessons, not one-off advice; tutor verification varies |
| **Free public legal aid** (Legal Aid Service of Georgia; courts’ free consultations, e.g. court.ge) | Free legal consultation for eligible people | Free, official | Only legal topics, eligibility limits; Wisecall should **point people to it** in law answers when relevant |

## What to copy, and what to do better

1. **Trust through transparency, never through tricks.** JustAnswer’s subscription problems show how quickly trust dies. Keep one clear
   price, no subscription, automatic refunds — and say it on every page (already true in the product).
2. **Verification is the product.** GrowthMentor’s “we accept 3%” and Clarity’s reviews both sell trust. Publish exactly how Wisecall
   verifies (ID, licence, human review, first answers checked) and show badges only for checks really passed (the code does).
3. **Reward mentors who bring their own buyers** (Intro 10%/30%, Topmate 10%/20%). A lower fee on questions that come through a
   mentor’s own profile link makes mentors market Wisecall for free.
4. **Written and asynchronous is Wisecall’s wedge** against call-based tools (Clarity, Intro): no scheduling, time-zone friendly for the
   diaspora, and the buyer keeps the answer.
5. **Compete with “free” on quality and accountability**, not price: a guaranteed answer within 7 days, from a verified person, or your
   money back.

## Recommended price ranges for the first mentors

These are **recommendations to test**, not market data. Anchors: tutoring in Tbilisi from about 50 ₾/hour (tutoroo.co), a written answer
takes a mentor roughly 20–40 minutes, and the financial model’s base case assumes a 40 ₾ average. Mentors set their own price
(5–2,000 ₾); suggest these on the mentor settings page as “typical prices in your field”.

| Field (niche keys in the product) | Suggested range | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Studying abroad, careers & HR, languages, teaching, parenting, cooking, fitness, sports coaching, music, film & photo, content creation, gaming | **15–40 ₾** | Students and young people are the main buyers; many free alternatives; volume matters more than price |
| Tech, data & AI, cybersecurity, design, marketing, writing, freelancing, e-commerce, engineering, architecture, science, tourism, real estate, wine & agriculture | **30–80 ₾** | Career- or income-changing questions; professionals’ time is scarcer |
| Business (founders), accounting & tax | **50–150 ₾** | Questions that save or make money; buyers are business owners |
| Medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, nursing, psychology, law, finance (regulated, licence checked) | **50–150 ₾** | Licensed professionals; answers are general guidance, not a consultation — keep prices below an in-person visit so the difference is clear |

Start the first mentors **in the lower half** of their range to get the first reviews, then let them raise prices (the product lets them
change it any time; paid questions keep their price).

## The 20% fee: keep it, with a launch offer for early mentors

- **Keep 20% as the standard fee.** It is comparable to MentorCruise (~20% on top), Topmate’s marketplace fee (20%) and below Intro (30%);
  Clarity (15%) and Topmate direct (10%) are lower but do not verify experts the way Wisecall does. The fee also has to cover the card fee
  (~2% [VERIFY]), verification, support and — depending on the accountant’s answer — VAT (`financial-model.md`).
- **Launch offer:** the **first 50 approved mentors pay 10% for their first 3 months** (or their first 20 answered questions). Reasons: it
  costs little — in the base scenario the first months have few questions, so the difference is small — it gives early mentors a concrete
  reason to join and to share their profile, and it is easy to explain. Announce the end date up front so the switch to 20% is not a
  surprise.
- **Later:** a **lower fee (e.g. 10%) on questions that come through a mentor’s own link** (Intro/Topmate model).
- **Code note:** the product has one global `PLATFORM_FEE_PERCENT`, but every booking already stores its own fee percent and amount, so a
  per-mentor or per-source fee is a contained change (write it in `docs/TODO-*` when you decide).
- **Model it first:** set `FeePct` on the Inputs sheet to 10% for the early months (copy a scenario) to see the cost of the offer.
