# What you must prepare yourself (GITA Startup Matching Grant)

Tick these before the call opens. Items marked [VERIFY] depend on the current call’s manual on grants.gov.ge — read it first.

## Eligibility
- [ ] **Read the current call’s manual / guidelines** on https://grants.gov.ge (the rules change between calls) [VERIFY]
- [ ] **Company age:** the enterprise must be **incorporated in Georgia within the last two years** — or apply as a natural person and
      **register the company before signing** if selected [VERIFY]
- [ ] **Age / legal capacity:** check whether the applicant or founders must be 18+ and whether the company’s legal representative must
      be a Georgian resident or citizen — **not found in the sources** [VERIFY in the manual]
- [ ] **No overlapping public grant** for the same costs; the **10% cash co-financing must not come from another public grant** [VERIFY]

## Company and money
- [ ] Register the company (usually an LLC, შპს) at the Public Service Hall / National Agency of Public Registry (napr.gov.ge); get the
      identification code — decide LLC vs individual entrepreneur **with the accountant** (VAT and profit-tax consequences,
      `docs/business/financial-model.md`)
- [ ] Open a business bank account (BOG or TBC) — the same bank as the e-commerce acquiring contract is simplest
- [ ] Proof of the **10% co-financing in cash** (bank statement, investor/founder commitment letter) [VERIFY what GITA accepts]
- [ ] Accountant engaged (quote → Inputs sheet of the model)
- [ ] Decide on **Innovative Startup Status** (innovationstatus.gov.ge) with the accountant

## Documents to write
- [ ] Business plan in the portal’s template — use `application-en.md` / `application-ka.md` as the source text
- [ ] Budget in the portal’s template — `budget.md` + `financial-model.xlsx` (set `GrantAmount`, `GrantMonth`, team cost)
- [ ] 12-month milestones with measurable targets (`application-en.md` §9) — fill in your targets
- [ ] Market numbers from **official sources** (Geostat, NAEC, ministries) — never estimates
- [ ] Team CVs; founders’ ID copies [VERIFY which documents]
- [ ] Pitch deck and possibly a short video pitch [VERIFY]
- [ ] Letters of intent from first mentors / partners (student organisations, professional communities) — strongest evidence of demand
      you can get before launch; only real ones

## Product evidence (you already have most of it)
- [ ] Screenshots or a short screen recording of the working product (sign-up, mentor application, asking and paying with the test bank)
- [ ] Waiting-list and mentor-application counts at the time of applying (real numbers from the admin panel)
- [ ] Links to the legal drafts (after lawyer review), the security audit and fixes, accessibility results

## After you apply
- [ ] Expect a shortlist and a **training week at the GITA Tech Park for finalists** (past rounds) — plan your time [VERIFY]
- [ ] Prepare a 5–7-minute pitch and answers on: innovation for Georgia, how you get the first 50 mentors, VAT model, data protection
