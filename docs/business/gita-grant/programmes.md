## 3. Innovative Startup Status — tax regime (not a grant)

- Reported benefits: a **10-year incentive scheme** with **0% profit and personal income tax in the early years** and lower payroll tax
  (5% instead of 20%) for companies GITA recognises as innovative (a new or significantly improved product, process or service).
- GITA checks the application in **15 working days**; the Government grants the status by decree; it is renewed **every year**, up to
  10 years.
- Where: **https://innovationstatus.gov.ge/en** · GITA information centre **+995 322 00 44 82** (from search results).
- Sources: https://expathub.ge/georgia-startup-tax-benefits-2026/ , https://www.deloryen.com/en/blog/georgia-innovative-startup-status-vs-virtual-zone-2026-tax-analysis ,
  https://ants.ge/en/georgias-innovation-law/ — **[VERIFY WITH ACCOUNTANT]** before counting on it.

## 4. Enterprise Georgia — Micro and Small Business Support (not a good fit)

Grants **up to 30,000 ₾**, 10% own contribution (5% in mountain regions), for adult Georgian citizens — but **at least 90% must be spent on
capital expenditure** (equipment), at most 10% on working capital. A software marketplace spends mostly on people, marketing and
services, so this does not fit. Source: https://www.enterprisegeorgia.gov.ge/en/news/micro-and-small-business-suppo [VERIFY]
